# Smart Contract Audits

UXUY core contracts are audited by independent third-party security firms, and the audit reports are publicly available.

Details: [https://docs.uxuy.com/security/audit/](https://docs.uxuy.com/security/audit/)

## Fund Security

- **Hot and cold wallet separation**: Platform funds are held separately in the Hot Vault and the Cold Vault. See [Contract and Infrastructure Addresses](./contracts-and-addresses.md) for details.
- **Insurance Fund**: Provides a buffer against bankruptcy losses from liquidations under extreme market conditions. See [Insurance Fund and Auto-Deleveraging (ADL)](../perpetuals/insurance-fund-and-adl.md) for details.
